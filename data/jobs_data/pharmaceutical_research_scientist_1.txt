46. Pharmaceutical Research Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Research Scientist conducts laboratory and clinical research to develop 
new drugs and improve existing medications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and conduct pharmaceutical research experiments 
• Study drug mechanisms and pharmacological effects 
• Analyze research data and prepare reports 
• Collaborate with multidisciplinary research teams 
• Publish research findings in scientific journals 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences 
• Strong research and analytical skills 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical research laboratories 
• Universities 
• Drug development companies
