18. Pharmacy Store Manager 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacy Store Manager oversees the daily operations of a pharmacy, ensuring efficient 
workflow, compliance, and excellent customer service. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage pharmacy operations and staff 
• Maintain medication inventory and ordering 
• Ensure regulatory compliance and safety standards 
• Handle financial management and sales performance 
• Resolve customer issues and complaints 
• Train and supervise pharmacy employees 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Management experience in pharmacy practice 
Work Settings 
• Retail pharmacy stores 
• Pharmacy chains 
• Hospital outpatient pharmacies
