56. Drug Development Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Drug Development Scientist works on the discovery and development of new 
pharmaceutical products from early research through clinical trials. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct preclinical and clinical research on new drugs 
• Design drug development strategies 
• Evaluate safety and efficacy data 
• Collaborate with regulatory and manufacturing teams 
• Support drug approval processes 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical research and development departments 
• Biotechnology companies 
• Drug discovery laboratories
