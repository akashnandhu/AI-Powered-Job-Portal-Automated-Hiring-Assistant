84. Aviation Medical Supply Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Aviation Medical Supply Pharmacist manages pharmaceutical supplies and medical kits 
used in aviation environments such as airlines, airports, and air ambulances. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Maintain medical kits used on commercial flights 
• Manage pharmaceutical inventory for aviation medical services 
• Ensure compliance with aviation health and safety regulations 
• Supply medications for air ambulance operations 
• Coordinate with airline medical departments 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Knowledge of aviation medical safety standards 
Work Settings 
• Airline medical departments 
• Air ambulance services 
• Airport medical facilities
