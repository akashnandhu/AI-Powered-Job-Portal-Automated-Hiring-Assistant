75. Procurement Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Procurement Pharmacist manages the purchasing and sourcing of pharmaceutical products 
and medical supplies for healthcare organizations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Identify and evaluate pharmaceutical suppliers 
• Negotiate purchase contracts and pricing agreements 
• Ensure procurement of quality medicines and supplies 
• Maintain procurement documentation and compliance 
• Monitor supplier performance and delivery schedules 
• Manage procurement budgets and cost control 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of procurement processes and pharmaceutical regulations 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals and healthcare systems 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Government healthcare procurement departments
