52. Clinical Research Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Research Scientist designs and conducts clinical studies to evaluate the safety and 
effectiveness of new drugs. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design clinical trial protocols 
• Analyze clinical trial data 
• Evaluate safety and efficacy of investigational drugs 
• Collaborate with regulatory and research teams 
• Publish research findings in scientific journals 
• Support regulatory submissions 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD / PharmD 
• Expertise in clinical research methodology 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Research institutes 
• Clinical research organizations
