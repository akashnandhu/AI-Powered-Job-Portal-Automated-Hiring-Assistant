68. Digital Health Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Digital Health Pharmacist works at the intersection of pharmacy and digital healthcare 
technologies to improve medication management through digital platforms. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop digital tools for medication adherence and patient monitoring 
• Support telehealth and remote patient care services 
• Evaluate digital health applications related to medication therapy 
• Collaborate with technology developers to improve health platforms 
• Ensure safe use of medications through digital systems 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of digital health technologies and telemedicine platforms 
Work Settings 
• Digital health startups 
• Telemedicine companies 
• Healthcare technology companies
