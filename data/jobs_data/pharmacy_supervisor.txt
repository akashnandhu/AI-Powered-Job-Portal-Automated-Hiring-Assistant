22. Pharmacy Supervisor 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacy Supervisor oversees pharmacy staff and ensures efficient daily operations within 
a pharmacy. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Supervise pharmacists, technicians, and assistants 
• Ensure accurate dispensing of medications 
• Monitor workflow and staff scheduling 
• Maintain compliance with pharmacy regulations 
• Provide training and performance evaluation 
• Ensure quality control in pharmacy services 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Experience in pharmacy operations 
Work Settings 
• Large retail pharmacies 
• Hospital outpatient pharmacies 
• Pharmacy chains
