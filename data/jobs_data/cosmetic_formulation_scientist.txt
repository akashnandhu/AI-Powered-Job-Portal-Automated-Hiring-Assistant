81. Cosmetic Formulation Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Cosmetic Formulation Scientist develops cosmetic and personal care products such as 
creams, lotions, shampoos, and skincare formulations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and develop cosmetic product formulations 
• Conduct stability and safety testing 
• Select appropriate cosmetic ingredients and preservatives 
• Ensure compliance with cosmetic regulations 
• Collaborate with marketing and product development teams 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutics or cosmetic science 
Work Settings 
• Cosmetic manufacturing companies 
• Personal care product companies 
• Cosmetic research laboratories
