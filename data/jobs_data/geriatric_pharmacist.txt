10. Geriatric Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Geriatric Pharmacist focuses on medication therapy for elderly patients. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage polypharmacy in elderly patients 
• Adjust medications based on age-related changes 
• Prevent drug interactions 
• Educate elderly patients and caregivers 
• Monitor medication adherence 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm with geriatric specialization 
Work Settings 
• Geriatric hospitals 
• Nursing homes 
• Long-term care facilities
