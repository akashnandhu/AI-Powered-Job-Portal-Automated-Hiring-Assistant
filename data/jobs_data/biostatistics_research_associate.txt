54. Biostatistics Research Associate 
Job Overview 
A Biostatistics Research Associate applies statistical methods to analyze clinical trial data 
and ensure scientific validity of research results in the field of Biostatistics. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design statistical analysis plans for clinical trials 
• Analyze research data using statistical software 
• Interpret clinical study results 
• Prepare statistical reports and presentations 
• Support researchers in data interpretation 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / MSc / PhD in biostatistics or related field 
• Knowledge of statistical software (SAS, R, SPSS) 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Clinical research organizations 
• Academic research institutions
