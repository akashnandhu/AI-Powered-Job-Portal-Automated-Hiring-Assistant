3. Ambulatory Care Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Ambulatory Care Pharmacist provides medication management for patients in outpatient 
settings, helping manage chronic diseases. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage chronic diseases (diabetes, hypertension, asthma) 
• Monitor long-term medication therapy 
• Provide lifestyle and medication counseling 
• Conduct medication therapy management (MTM) 
• Monitor lab values and adjust medications 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD or M.Pharm 
• Clinical training in outpatient care 
Work Settings 
• Outpatient clinics 
• Community health centers 
• Specialty care clinics
