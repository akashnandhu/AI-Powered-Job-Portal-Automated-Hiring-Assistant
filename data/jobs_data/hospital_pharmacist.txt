2. Hospital Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Hospital Pharmacist manages medication dispensing and ensures proper pharmaceutical 
care for hospitalized patients. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Dispense medications prescribed by doctors 
• Maintain hospital drug inventory 
• Verify prescriptions and medication orders 
• Monitor drug therapy effectiveness 
• Provide drug information to healthcare staff 
• Ensure safe storage of medicines 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Pharmacy Council registration 
Work Settings 
• Government hospitals 
• Private hospitals 
• Specialty hospitals
