5. Oncology Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Oncology Pharmacist specializes in medications used to treat cancer and supports patients 
undergoing chemotherapy. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Prepare and dispense chemotherapy drugs 
• Monitor cancer treatment regimens 
• Manage side effects of cancer medications 
• Ensure safe handling of hazardous drugs 
• Educate patients about chemotherapy 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm in Oncology Pharmacy 
• Certification in oncology pharmacy (preferred) 
Work Settings 
• Cancer hospitals 
• Oncology clinics 
• Research centers
