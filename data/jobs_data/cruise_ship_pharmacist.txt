83. Cruise Ship Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Cruise Ship Pharmacist manages the onboard pharmacy and provides pharmaceutical care 
to passengers and crew members traveling on cruise ships. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Dispense prescription and over-the-counter medications onboard 
• Provide medication counseling to passengers and crew 
• Manage onboard pharmacy inventory and medical supplies 
• Assist ship doctors in managing medical emergencies 
• Ensure proper storage of medicines in maritime environments 
• Maintain medical records and regulatory compliance 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Valid pharmacist license 
• Maritime or travel medicine training (preferred) 
Work Settings 
• International cruise ships 
• Maritime healthcare services 
• Cruise medical centers
