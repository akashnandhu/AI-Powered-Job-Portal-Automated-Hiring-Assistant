25. Production Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Production Pharmacist manages drug manufacturing operations to ensure medicines are 
produced safely, efficiently, and according to regulatory standards. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Supervise drug production processes 
• Monitor manufacturing equipment and facilities 
• Ensure batch production records are accurate 
• Maintain production schedules and timelines 
• Ensure compliance with quality standards and GMP 
• Coordinate with quality control and packaging teams 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Experience in pharmaceutical production 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical production units 
• Manufacturing plants
