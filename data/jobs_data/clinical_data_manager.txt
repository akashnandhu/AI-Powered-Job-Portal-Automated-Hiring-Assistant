55. Clinical Data Manager 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Data Manager oversees the collection, management, and quality control of data 
generated during clinical trials. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design clinical trial data management systems 
• Ensure accuracy and integrity of clinical data 
• Monitor data collection from research sites 
• Maintain clinical trial databases 
• Ensure compliance with regulatory data standards 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Knowledge of clinical data management systems 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Contract research organizations 
• Clinical trial management companies
