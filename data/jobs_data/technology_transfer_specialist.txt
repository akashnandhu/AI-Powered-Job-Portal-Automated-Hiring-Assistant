33. Technology Transfer Specialist 
Job Overview 
A Technology Transfer Specialist manages the transfer of pharmaceutical manufacturing 
processes from research laboratories to commercial production facilities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Coordinate transfer of drug formulations to manufacturing units 
• Ensure smooth scale-up of production processes 
• Prepare technology transfer documentation 
• Train manufacturing staff on new processes 
• Ensure regulatory compliance during transfer 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutics or related field 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical manufacturing companies 
• R&D and production departments
