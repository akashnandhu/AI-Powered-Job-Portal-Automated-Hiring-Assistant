70. Telepharmacy Consultant 
Job Overview 
A Telepharmacy Consultant provides pharmaceutical care remotely using telecommunication 
technologies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Provide remote medication counseling to patients 
• Review prescriptions and ensure safe medication use 
• Support rural or remote pharmacies through telepharmacy services 
• Monitor medication adherence using digital tools 
• Collaborate with physicians and healthcare providers remotely 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm / B.Pharm 
• Training in telehealth and remote patient care 
Work Settings 
• Telehealth platforms 
• Online pharmacy services 
• Remote healthcare clinics
