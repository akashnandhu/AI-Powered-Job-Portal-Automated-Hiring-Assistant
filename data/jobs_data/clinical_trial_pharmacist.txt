51. Clinical Trial Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Trial Pharmacist manages investigational drugs used in clinical trials and ensures 
safe handling, dispensing, and documentation during research studies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage investigational drug supplies for clinical trials 
• Prepare and dispense study medications according to protocols 
• Maintain drug accountability records 
• Ensure proper storage and labeling of clinical trial drugs 
• Educate research staff on investigational drug usage 
• Ensure compliance with clinical trial regulations and guidelines 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Training in clinical trial management 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals conducting clinical trials 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Contract Research Organizations (CROs)
