26. Manufacturing Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Manufacturing Pharmacist focuses on the technical aspects of drug production, ensuring 
proper formulation, equipment operation, and compliance with regulatory guidelines. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage manufacturing processes for pharmaceuticals 
• Monitor equipment calibration and operation 
• Ensure proper handling of raw materials and active ingredients 
• Optimize manufacturing processes for efficiency 
• Maintain documentation and production records 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of manufacturing technologies 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities 
• Biotech production plants
