82. Herbal / Ayurvedic Drug Researcher 
Job Overview 
A Herbal or Ayurvedic Drug Researcher studies medicinal plants and traditional remedies to 
develop herbal medicines within the field of Ayurveda and herbal pharmacology. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Research medicinal plants and natural compounds 
• Develop herbal drug formulations 
• Conduct pharmacological studies on herbal medicines 
• Ensure safety and quality of herbal products 
• Publish research on traditional medicine and herbal therapies 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PhD in pharmacognosy or herbal drug research 
Work Settings 
• Herbal pharmaceutical companies 
• Ayurvedic medicine research institutes 
• Natural product research laboratories
