36. Drug Control Officer 
Job Overview 
A Drug Control Officer supervises and enforces pharmaceutical regulations across regions to 
ensure safe distribution and sale of medicines. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Oversee enforcement of drug control laws 
• Supervise drug inspectors and regulatory teams 
• Monitor pharmaceutical supply chains 
• Investigate violations of drug regulations 
• Coordinate regulatory actions against illegal drug distribution 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Government regulatory training 
Work Settings 
• Government drug control departments 
• National drug regulatory agencies
