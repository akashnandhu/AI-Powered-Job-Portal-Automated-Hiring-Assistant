86. Humanitarian Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Humanitarian Pharmacist works in disaster relief and global health programs to ensure 
access to essential medicines in crisis situations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage medicine supply in disaster-affected regions 
• Support emergency healthcare programs 
• Coordinate pharmaceutical logistics for relief operations 
• Ensure safe and appropriate medication use in humanitarian settings 
• Train local healthcare workers on medication management 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Training in humanitarian health or emergency medicine 
Work Settings 
• International humanitarian organizations 
• Disaster relief missions 
• Refugee healthcare programs 
Example organization: World Health Organization
