21. Consultant Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Consultant Pharmacist provides expert advice on medication management, drug safety, and 
pharmaceutical policies to healthcare organizations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Review medication therapy for patients 
• Provide recommendations for optimizing drug treatment 
• Conduct medication audits and compliance checks 
• Train healthcare staff on medication safety 
• Develop pharmacy policies and procedures 
• Support long-term care facilities with medication management 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Advanced clinical or consulting experience 
Work Settings 
• Healthcare consulting firms 
• Nursing homes 
• Long-term care facilities 
• Healthcare organizations
