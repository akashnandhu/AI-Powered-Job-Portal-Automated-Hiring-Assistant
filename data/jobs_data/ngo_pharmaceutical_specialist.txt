87. NGO Pharmaceutical Specialist 
Job Overview 
An NGO Pharmaceutical Specialist manages pharmaceutical programs and medication 
distribution for non-governmental healthcare organizations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop pharmaceutical policies for NGO health programs 
• Manage procurement and distribution of medicines 
• Monitor drug safety and quality in community programs 
• Support public health initiatives and vaccination campaigns 
• Train healthcare workers in medication management 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Experience in public health or global health programs 
Work Settings 
• International NGOs 
• Public health organizations 
• Global healthcare programs
