60. Pharmaceutical Marketing Manager 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Marketing Manager develops marketing strategies to promote 
pharmaceutical products to healthcare professionals and healthcare institutions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop marketing campaigns for pharmaceutical products 
• Conduct market research and analyze industry trends 
• Manage brand positioning and product promotion 
• Coordinate with sales teams to achieve marketing goals 
• Organize medical conferences and promotional events 
• Monitor marketing performance metrics 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / MBA in Marketing or Pharmaceutical Management 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Healthcare marketing agencies 
• Biotechnology firms
