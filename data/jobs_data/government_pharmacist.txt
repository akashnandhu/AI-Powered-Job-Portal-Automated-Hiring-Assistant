40. Government Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Government Pharmacist works in public healthcare institutions to provide pharmaceutical 
services and ensure proper distribution of medicines in government healthcare programs. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Dispense medications in government hospitals and clinics 
• Manage drug supply and inventory for public health programs 
• Ensure quality and safety of medicines supplied to patients 
• Support national health initiatives and vaccination programs 
• Maintain pharmaceutical records and documentation 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Government recruitment examination 
Work Settings 
• Government hospitals 
• Public health centers 
• Government medical supply departments
