47. Clinical Research Associate 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Research Associate (CRA) monitors clinical trials to ensure they are conducted 
according to regulatory standards and study protocols. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Monitor clinical trial sites and research activities 
• Ensure compliance with clinical research regulations 
• Verify accuracy of clinical trial data 
• Review trial documentation and reports 
• Communicate with investigators and sponsors 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Training in clinical research 
Work Settings 
• Contract Research Organizations (CROs) 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Clinical trial management organizations
