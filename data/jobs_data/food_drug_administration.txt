42. Food & Drug Administration 
Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Food & Drug Administration Pharmacist works within regulatory authorities to evaluate 
pharmaceutical products, approve drug applications, and ensure medication safety. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Evaluate pharmaceutical product applications 
• Review clinical trial data and safety reports 
• Ensure compliance with national drug regulations 
• Approve or reject drug marketing applications 
• Monitor post-marketing drug safety 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences 
• Regulatory affairs expertise 
Work Settings 
• National drug regulatory agencies 
• Government health departments
