63. Pharmaceutical Sales Manager 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Sales Manager leads sales teams responsible for promoting and selling 
pharmaceutical products to hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage pharmaceutical sales teams 
• Develop sales strategies and targets 
• Monitor sales performance and revenue growth 
• Train sales representatives 
• Build relationships with healthcare providers and distributors 
• Analyze market trends and sales data 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / MBA in Marketing or Sales 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Medical product companies 
• Healthcare distribution firms
