65. Medical Advisor 
Job Overview 
A Medical Advisor provides expert medical and scientific guidance to pharmaceutical 
companies regarding drug development, marketing strategies, and regulatory compliance. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Provide scientific advice on pharmaceutical products 
• Review clinical research and medical literature 
• Support regulatory submissions and medical communications 
• Train internal teams on medical information 
• Ensure ethical and scientific accuracy in promotional materials 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences 
• Experience in medical affairs or clinical research 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Biotechnology firms 
• Medical advisory departments
