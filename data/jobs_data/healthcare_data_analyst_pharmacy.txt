67. Healthcare Data Analyst (Pharmacy) 
Job Overview 
A Healthcare Data Analyst in pharmacy analyzes healthcare and medication data to improve 
clinical outcomes and operational efficiency. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Analyze large datasets related to medication usage 
• Identify trends in drug prescriptions and patient outcomes 
• Develop dashboards and data visualization reports 
• Support healthcare decision-making through data insights 
• Monitor drug safety and treatment effectiveness 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Skills in data analytics tools (Python, R, SQL, Power BI) 
Work Settings 
• Healthcare organizations 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Health analytics firms
