4. Critical Care Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Critical Care Pharmacist specializes in medication therapy for critically ill patients in 
intensive care units (ICU). 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage complex medication regimens for ICU patients 
• Monitor life-support medications 
• Provide emergency drug dosing recommendations 
• Prevent medication errors in critical care 
• Participate in ICU clinical rounds 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD with critical care residency 
• Advanced clinical training 
Work Settings 
• Intensive Care Units (ICU) 
• Trauma centers 
• Emergency hospitals
