38. Pharmacovigilance Officer 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacovigilance Officer monitors the safety of medicines after they are released to the 
market and tracks adverse drug reactions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Collect and analyze adverse drug reaction (ADR) reports 
• Monitor drug safety data from hospitals and clinics 
• Report safety concerns to regulatory authorities 
• Evaluate risks associated with pharmaceutical products 
• Maintain drug safety databases 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Training in pharmacovigilance and drug safety monitoring 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Drug safety monitoring centers 
• Regulatory agencies
