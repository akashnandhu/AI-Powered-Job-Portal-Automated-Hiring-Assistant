35. Drug Inspector 
Job Overview 
A Drug Inspector is responsible for ensuring that pharmaceutical products are manufactured, 
stored, and sold according to national drug laws and safety regulations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Inspect pharmaceutical manufacturing units and pharmacies 
• Ensure compliance with drug laws and regulatory standards 
• Collect drug samples for laboratory testing 
• Investigate complaints regarding drug quality or safety 
• Monitor illegal drug sales and counterfeit medicines 
• Prepare inspection reports and regulatory documentation 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / Pharmaceutical Sciences degree 
• Government recruitment examination (varies by country) 
Work Settings 
• Drug regulatory authorities 
• Government health departments 
• Pharmaceutical inspection divisions
