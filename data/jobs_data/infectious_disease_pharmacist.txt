8. Infectious Disease Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Infectious Disease Pharmacist specializes in antimicrobial therapy and infection control. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage antibiotic therapy programs 
• Monitor antimicrobial resistance 
• Optimize antibiotic selection and dosing 
• Participate in infection control committees 
• Educate healthcare staff on antimicrobial stewardship 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD with infectious disease specialization 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals 
• Infectious disease clinics 
• Public health organizations
