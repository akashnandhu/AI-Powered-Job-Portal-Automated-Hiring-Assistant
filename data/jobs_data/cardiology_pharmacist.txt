9. Cardiology Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Cardiology Pharmacist specializes in medications used to treat heart conditions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage medications for heart diseases 
• Monitor anticoagulant therapy 
• Adjust cardiac medication doses 
• Educate patients about cardiovascular drugs 
• Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol therapies 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm with cardiology training 
Work Settings 
• Cardiac hospitals 
• Cardiology clinics 
• Cardiac care units
