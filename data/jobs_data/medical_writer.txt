53. Medical Writer 
Job Overview 
A Medical Writer prepares scientific and regulatory documents related to pharmaceutical 
research, clinical trials, and drug approvals. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Write clinical study reports and research manuscripts 
• Prepare regulatory documents for drug approvals 
• Develop scientific articles and publications 
• Create educational materials for healthcare professionals 
• Review and edit scientific documents 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Strong scientific writing skills 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Medical communication agencies 
• Research institutions
