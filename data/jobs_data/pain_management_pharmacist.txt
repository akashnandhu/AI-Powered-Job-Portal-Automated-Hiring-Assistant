14. Pain Management Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Pain Management Pharmacist specializes in medications used to treat acute and chronic 
pain. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Optimize pain management therapies 
• Monitor opioid therapy 
• Prevent drug misuse and dependency 
• Collaborate with pain specialists 
• Educate patients on pain medications 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Pain management training 
Work Settings 
• Pain clinics 
• Hospitals 
• Rehabilitation centers
