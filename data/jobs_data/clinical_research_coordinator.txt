48. Clinical Research Coordinator 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Research Coordinator manages day-to-day operations of clinical trials and ensures 
studies run smoothly. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Coordinate clinical trial activities 
• Recruit and screen research participants 
• Maintain trial documentation and records 
• Ensure ethical and regulatory compliance 
• Communicate with investigators and sponsors 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Clinical research training 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals 
• Research institutions 
• Clinical trial centers
