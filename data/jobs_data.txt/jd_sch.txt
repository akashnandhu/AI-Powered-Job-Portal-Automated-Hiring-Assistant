1. Clinical Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Pharmacist works directly with physicians, nurses, and patients to ensure 
medications are used safely and effectively. They review prescriptions, optimize drug 
therapy, and provide medication counseling. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Review medication orders for accuracy and safety 
• Monitor patient drug therapy and outcomes 
• Provide medication counseling to patients 
• Prevent adverse drug reactions and drug interactions 
• Collaborate with healthcare teams to develop treatment plans 
• Participate in clinical rounds 
• Adjust medication dosages based on patient conditions 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD or M.Pharm (Clinical Pharmacy) 
• Pharmacy license/registration 
• Residency training (preferred) 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals 
• Specialty clinics 
• Academic medical centers
