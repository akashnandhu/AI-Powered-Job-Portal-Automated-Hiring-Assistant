34. Packaging Development Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Packaging Development Scientist designs and tests packaging systems to protect 
pharmaceutical products and ensure regulatory compliance. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop packaging materials for pharmaceutical products 
• Conduct stability and compatibility studies 
• Ensure packaging meets regulatory and safety standards 
• Improve packaging efficiency and sustainability 
• Collaborate with production and marketing teams 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm with packaging or formulation knowledge 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical manufacturing companies 
• Packaging development laboratories
