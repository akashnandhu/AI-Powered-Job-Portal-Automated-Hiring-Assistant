57. Clinical Quality Specialist 
Job Overview 
A Clinical Quality Specialist ensures that clinical trials follow strict regulatory and ethical 
standards. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Monitor compliance with clinical trial regulations 
• Conduct quality audits of clinical trial processes 
• Review clinical trial documentation 
• Identify and correct protocol deviations 
• Ensure adherence to Good Clinical Practice (GCP) standards 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Knowledge of clinical quality management systems 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Clinical research organizations 
• Regulatory compliance departments
