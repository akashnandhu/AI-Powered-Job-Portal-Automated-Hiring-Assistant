50. Pharmacology Researcher 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacology Researcher studies how drugs interact with biological systems to improve 
drug safety and effectiveness in the field of Pharmacology. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Study mechanisms of drug action 
• Conduct laboratory experiments on drug effects 
• Analyze pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics 
• Develop safer and more effective medications 
• Publish research findings 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmacology or pharmaceutical sciences 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical research institutes 
• Universities 
• Drug development laboratories
