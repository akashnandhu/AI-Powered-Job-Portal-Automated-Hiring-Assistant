59. Pharmaceutical Product Manager 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Product Manager is responsible for planning, developing, and managing 
pharmaceutical products throughout their lifecycle. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop product strategies and marketing plans 
• Conduct market research and competitor analysis 
• Coordinate product launches 
• Monitor product performance and sales 
• Collaborate with research, regulatory, and sales teams 
• Develop promotional materials for healthcare professionals 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / MBA (Pharma Management preferred) 
• Knowledge of pharmaceutical marketing and product management 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Biotechnology firms 
• Healthcare product companies
