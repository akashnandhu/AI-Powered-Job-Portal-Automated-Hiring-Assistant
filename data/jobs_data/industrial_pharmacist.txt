24. Industrial Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Industrial Pharmacist works in pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities and oversees the 
production, quality, and regulatory compliance of medicines. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Supervise pharmaceutical manufacturing processes 
• Ensure compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) 
• Monitor production quality and efficiency 
• Maintain proper documentation of manufacturing activities 
• Ensure safe handling of pharmaceutical ingredients 
• Coordinate with production, quality, and regulatory teams 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of pharmaceutical manufacturing and GMP 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical manufacturing plants 
• Drug production companies 
• Biopharmaceutical industries
