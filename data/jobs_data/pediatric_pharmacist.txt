6. Pediatric Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Pediatric Pharmacist focuses on medication therapy for infants, children, and adolescents. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Adjust drug doses based on age and weight 
• Prepare pediatric formulations 
• Monitor drug safety in children 
• Educate parents about medications 
• Prevent pediatric medication errors 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Pediatric pharmacy training 
Work Settings 
• Children's hospitals 
• Pediatric clinics 
• Neonatal intensive care units
