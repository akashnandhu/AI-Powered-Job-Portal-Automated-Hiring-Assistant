77. Nuclear Pharmacy Specialist 
Job Overview 
A Nuclear Pharmacy Specialist prepares and dispenses radioactive pharmaceutical 
compounds used in diagnostic imaging and treatment procedures within the field of Nuclear 
Medicine. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Prepare radiopharmaceuticals used in diagnostic imaging 
• Ensure safe handling and storage of radioactive materials 
• Maintain radiation safety protocols 
• Dispense nuclear medicine drugs for medical procedures 
• Monitor radiation exposure and compliance with safety regulations 
• Collaborate with radiologists and nuclear medicine physicians 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Specialized training or certification in nuclear pharmacy 
Work Settings 
• Nuclear medicine departments in hospitals 
• Diagnostic imaging centers 
• Radiopharmaceutical manufacturing facilities
