7. Emergency Medicine Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Emergency Medicine Pharmacist supports rapid medication therapy decisions in 
emergency departments. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Provide drug information during emergencies 
• Prepare emergency medications 
• Assist with resuscitation drugs 
• Monitor high-risk medications 
• Prevent medication errors in emergency care 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD with emergency medicine training 
Work Settings 
• Emergency departments 
• Trauma centers 
• Disaster response units
