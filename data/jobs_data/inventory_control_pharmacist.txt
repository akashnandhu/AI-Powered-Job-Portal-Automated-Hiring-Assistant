76. Inventory Control Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
An Inventory Control Pharmacist manages pharmaceutical inventory to ensure accurate stock 
levels and prevent medication shortages or wastage. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Monitor inventory levels of pharmaceutical products 
• Maintain stock records and inventory databases 
• Implement inventory management systems 
• Prevent expired or obsolete medication stock 
• Conduct regular inventory audits 
• Coordinate with procurement and distribution teams 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of inventory management systems 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals and healthcare facilities 
• Pharmaceutical warehouses 
• Drug distribution companies
