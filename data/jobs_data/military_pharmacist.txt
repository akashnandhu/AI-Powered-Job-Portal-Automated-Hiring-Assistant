85. Military Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Military Pharmacist provides pharmaceutical services to armed forces personnel and 
manages medication supply for military medical units. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Dispense medications to military personnel 
• Manage pharmaceutical supply in military hospitals and field units 
• Support medical teams during military operations and emergencies 
• Maintain medical readiness for combat or disaster situations 
• Ensure safe handling and distribution of medications in military environments 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Military medical training 
Work Settings 
• Military hospitals 
• Armed forces medical units 
• Defense healthcare services 
Example organization: Indian Armed Forces
