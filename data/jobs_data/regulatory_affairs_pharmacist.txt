37. Regulatory Affairs Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Regulatory Affairs Pharmacist ensures that pharmaceutical products meet regulatory 
requirements before they are approved for sale in the market. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Prepare regulatory submissions for drug approvals 
• Maintain compliance with regulatory guidelines 
• Communicate with regulatory agencies regarding product approval 
• Monitor changes in pharmaceutical regulations 
• Prepare documentation for product registration 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of regulatory affairs and drug approval processes 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Regulatory consulting firms 
• Drug regulatory authorities
