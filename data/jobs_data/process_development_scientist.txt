31. Process Development Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Process Development Scientist focuses on optimizing manufacturing processes for 
pharmaceutical production. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop scalable manufacturing processes 
• Improve production efficiency and product quality 
• Conduct process validation studies 
• Troubleshoot production problems 
• Collaborate with manufacturing and R&D teams 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutics or chemical engineering 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical R&D centers 
• Manufacturing plants
