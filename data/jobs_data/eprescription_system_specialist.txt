69. E-Prescription System Specialist 
Job Overview 
An E-Prescription System Specialist designs and manages electronic prescribing systems that 
allow healthcare providers to send prescriptions digitally to pharmacies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Implement and maintain electronic prescribing platforms 
• Ensure secure transmission of prescription data 
• Integrate e-prescription systems with hospital information systems 
• Monitor system performance and troubleshoot issues 
• Train healthcare providers on electronic prescribing tools 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of healthcare IT systems and electronic health records 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals and healthcare networks 
• Health IT companies 
• Pharmacy software development firms
