73. Drug Distribution Manager 
Job Overview 
A Drug Distribution Manager supervises the distribution of pharmaceutical products to 
hospitals, pharmacies, and healthcare providers. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage pharmaceutical distribution networks 
• Ensure timely delivery of medicines to healthcare facilities 
• Monitor compliance with drug storage and transportation standards 
• Coordinate with wholesalers, retailers, and logistics providers 
• Track distribution performance and logistics efficiency 
• Maintain distribution documentation and regulatory compliance 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of pharmaceutical distribution regulations 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical distribution companies 
• Wholesale drug suppliers 
• Pharmaceutical logistics firms
