74. Pharmaceutical Warehouse Manager 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Warehouse Manager is responsible for the storage, handling, and inventory 
management of pharmaceutical products in warehouses. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage pharmaceutical warehouse operations 
• Ensure proper storage conditions for medicines (temperature, humidity control) 
• Supervise warehouse staff and logistics teams 
• Maintain accurate inventory records 
• Implement safety and regulatory compliance procedures 
• Coordinate incoming and outgoing shipments 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / Logistics management training 
• Experience in warehouse or logistics operations 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical warehouses 
• Drug distribution centers 
• Medical supply companies
