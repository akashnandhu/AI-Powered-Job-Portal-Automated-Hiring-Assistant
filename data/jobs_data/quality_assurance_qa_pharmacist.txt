27. Quality Assurance (QA) Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Quality Assurance Pharmacist ensures pharmaceutical products meet required quality 
standards before reaching the market. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and implement quality assurance systems 
• Conduct internal audits and inspections 
• Ensure compliance with regulatory standards such as GMP 
• Review manufacturing documentation 
• Investigate deviations and product complaints 
• Approve final product release 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of pharmaceutical quality systems 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Quality assurance departments
