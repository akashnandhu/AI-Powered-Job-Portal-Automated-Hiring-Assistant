72. Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Manager 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Manager oversees the entire supply chain process for 
pharmaceutical products, ensuring medicines are delivered efficiently from manufacturers to 
pharmacies and healthcare facilities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Plan and manage pharmaceutical supply chain operations 
• Coordinate procurement, production, and distribution activities 
• Monitor inventory levels across supply chain networks 
• Ensure compliance with pharmaceutical storage and transportation regulations 
• Optimize logistics processes to reduce costs and improve efficiency 
• Manage relationships with suppliers, distributors, and logistics partners 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / MBA in Supply Chain Management 
• Experience in pharmaceutical logistics and distribution 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Drug distribution companies 
• Healthcare supply chain organizations
