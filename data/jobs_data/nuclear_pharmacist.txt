13. Nuclear Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Nuclear Pharmacist prepares and dispenses radioactive drugs used in diagnostic imaging 
and treatment in the field of Nuclear Medicine. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Prepare radiopharmaceuticals 
• Ensure radiation safety protocols 
• Dispense radioactive medications 
• Monitor dosage accuracy 
• Maintain regulatory compliance 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD or M.Pharm 
• Certification in nuclear pharmacy 
Work Settings 
• Nuclear medicine departments 
• Diagnostic imaging centers
