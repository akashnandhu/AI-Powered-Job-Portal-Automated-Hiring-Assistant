32. Validation Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Validation Pharmacist ensures pharmaceutical equipment, systems, and processes meet 
regulatory requirements before production begins. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Perform equipment and process validation 
• Prepare validation protocols and reports 
• Ensure compliance with regulatory standards 
• Conduct installation, operational, and performance qualification 
• Monitor validated systems regularly 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of validation procedures 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical manufacturing companies 
• Quality assurance departments
