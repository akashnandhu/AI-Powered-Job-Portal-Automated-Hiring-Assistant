41. Public Health Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Public Health Pharmacist works to improve community health by managing medication
related public health programs and policies in the field of Public Health. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement public health medication programs 
• Support disease prevention initiatives 
• Monitor drug distribution during public health emergencies 
• Conduct community health education programs 
• Collaborate with healthcare professionals to improve medication access 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Additional training in public health (MPH preferred) 
Work Settings 
• Government health departments 
• Public health organizations 
• International health agencies
