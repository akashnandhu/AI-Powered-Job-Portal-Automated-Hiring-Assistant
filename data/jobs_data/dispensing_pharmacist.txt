23. Dispensing Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Dispensing Pharmacist is responsible for accurately preparing and dispensing prescribed 
medications to patients. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Interpret prescriptions from physicians 
• Dispense correct medications and dosages 
• Label medicines and provide instructions 
• Maintain medication records and documentation 
• Check for drug interactions and allergies 
• Provide patient counseling 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Pharmacist registration 
Work Settings 
• Community pharmacies 
• Hospital pharmacies 
• Retail drugstores
