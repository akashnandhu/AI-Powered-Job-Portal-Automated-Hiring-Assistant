19. Chain Pharmacy Manager 
Job Overview 
A Chain Pharmacy Manager supervises multiple pharmacy branches within a retail chain and 
ensures standardized operations across locations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage operations across several pharmacy stores 
• Develop operational strategies and policies 
• Monitor sales, profits, and performance metrics 
• Ensure regulatory compliance across branches 
• Recruit and train pharmacy staff 
• Implement marketing and customer service strategies 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Leadership and management experience 
Work Settings 
• National pharmacy chains 
• Corporate pharmacy organizations
