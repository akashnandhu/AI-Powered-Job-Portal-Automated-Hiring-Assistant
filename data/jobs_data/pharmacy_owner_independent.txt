20. Pharmacy Owner / Independent 
Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacy Owner operates and manages an independent pharmacy business, providing 
healthcare services and pharmaceutical products to the community. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Establish and manage pharmacy business operations 
• Procure medicines and pharmaceutical products 
• Provide prescription dispensing and counseling 
• Manage finances, accounting, and licensing 
• Ensure compliance with drug laws and regulations 
• Build relationships with local healthcare providers 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Pharmacy license and business registration 
Work Settings 
• Independent community pharmacies 
• Private drugstores
