49. Principal Investigator (Clinical Trials) 
Job Overview 
A Principal Investigator (PI) leads and oversees clinical trials to evaluate new drugs, medical 
devices, or treatment protocols. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and lead clinical trial studies 
• Ensure ethical conduct of research 
• Supervise research teams and staff 
• Analyze clinical trial results 
• Report study findings to regulatory authorities 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / PhD / MD with clinical research experience 
• Extensive knowledge of clinical trial regulations 
Work Settings 
• Research hospitals 
• Universities 
• Pharmaceutical research organizations
