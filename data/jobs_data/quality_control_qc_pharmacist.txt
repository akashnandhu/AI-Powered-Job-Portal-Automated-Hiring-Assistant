28. Quality Control (QC) Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Quality Control Pharmacist performs laboratory testing to verify the quality, purity, and 
safety of pharmaceutical products. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct chemical and microbiological testing 
• Analyze raw materials and finished products 
• Operate laboratory instruments (HPLC, GC, etc.) 
• Maintain laboratory records and reports 
• Ensure compliance with regulatory standards 
• Identify product defects or contamination 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm 
• Laboratory testing experience 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical QC laboratories 
• Research laboratories
