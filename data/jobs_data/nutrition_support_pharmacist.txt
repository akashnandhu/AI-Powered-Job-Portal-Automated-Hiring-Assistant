15. Nutrition Support Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Nutrition Support Pharmacist specializes in preparing and managing parenteral and enteral 
nutrition therapies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Prepare total parenteral nutrition (TPN) 
• Monitor nutritional therapy outcomes 
• Adjust electrolyte and nutrient levels 
• Collaborate with dietitians and physicians 
• Ensure sterile compounding practices 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Specialized nutrition support training 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals 
• Intensive care units 
• Specialty nutrition clinics
