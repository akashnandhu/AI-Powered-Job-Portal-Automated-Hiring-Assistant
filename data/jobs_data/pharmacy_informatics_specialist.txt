66. Pharmacy Informatics Specialist 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacy Informatics Specialist integrates pharmacy practice with information technology 
to improve medication management systems and healthcare workflows. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and maintain pharmacy information systems 
• Implement electronic medication management systems 
• Optimize electronic prescribing and dispensing workflows 
• Analyze medication-use data for safety improvements 
• Train healthcare staff on pharmacy software systems 
• Ensure compliance with healthcare data standards 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Additional training in health informatics or healthcare IT 
Work Settings 
• Hospitals and healthcare systems 
• Health IT companies 
• Pharmacy management software companies
