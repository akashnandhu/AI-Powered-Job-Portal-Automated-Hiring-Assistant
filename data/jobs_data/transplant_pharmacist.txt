12. Transplant Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Transplant Pharmacist manages medications for patients undergoing organ transplantation. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage immunosuppressive therapy 
• Prevent transplant rejection 
• Monitor drug interactions 
• Counsel transplant patients 
• Monitor therapeutic drug levels 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD with transplant pharmacy training 
Work Settings 
• Organ transplant centers 
• Specialty hospitals
