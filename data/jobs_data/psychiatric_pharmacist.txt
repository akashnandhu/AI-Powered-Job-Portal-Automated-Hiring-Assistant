11. Psychiatric Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Psychiatric Pharmacist specializes in medications used to treat mental health disorders 
within the field of Psychiatry. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage psychiatric medications 
• Monitor side effects of antipsychotics and antidepressants 
• Assist psychiatrists in treatment planning 
• Counsel patients about medication adherence 
• Monitor drug interactions with psychiatric drugs 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Psychiatric pharmacy training 
Work Settings 
• Mental health hospitals 
• Psychiatric clinics 
• Rehabilitation centers
