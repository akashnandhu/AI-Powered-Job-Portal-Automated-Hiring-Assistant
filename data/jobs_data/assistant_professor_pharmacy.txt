45. Assistant Professor (Pharmacy) 
Job Overview 
An Assistant Professor is an entry-level academic faculty member responsible for teaching 
pharmacy courses and beginning independent research activities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Teach pharmacy subjects to undergraduate students 
• Conduct laboratory sessions and practical training 
• Assist in research projects and publications 
• Mentor students academically 
• Participate in academic conferences and workshops 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm or PhD in Pharmacy 
• Qualification in national eligibility tests (in some countries) 
Work Settings 
• Pharmacy colleges 
• Universities 
• Academic research centers
