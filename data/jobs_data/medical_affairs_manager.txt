58. Medical Affairs Manager 
Job Overview 
A Medical Affairs Manager bridges the gap between pharmaceutical companies and 
healthcare professionals by providing scientific and medical information about 
pharmaceutical products. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Provide scientific support for pharmaceutical products 
• Communicate clinical data to healthcare professionals 
• Support medical education and training programs 
• Manage medical information and scientific publications 
• Collaborate with research, marketing, and regulatory teams 
• Ensure compliance with medical and ethical guidelines 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences 
• Experience in medical affairs or clinical research 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Biotechnology companies 
• Medical affairs departments
