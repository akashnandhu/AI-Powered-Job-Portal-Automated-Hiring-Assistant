64. Business Development Manager 
(Pharma) 
Job Overview 
A Business Development Manager identifies new business opportunities and partnerships to 
expand pharmaceutical company operations and product reach. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Identify new market opportunities 
• Develop strategic partnerships with healthcare organizations 
• Negotiate business agreements and collaborations 
• Conduct market analysis and competitor research 
• Support product expansion and international growth 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / MBA in Business Administration 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Biotechnology companies 
• Healthcare corporations
