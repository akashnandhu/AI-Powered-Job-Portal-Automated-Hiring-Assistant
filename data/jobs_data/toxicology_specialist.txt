80. Toxicology Specialist 
Job Overview 
A Toxicology Specialist studies the harmful effects of chemicals, drugs, and toxins on 
biological systems in the field of Toxicology. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Investigate drug toxicity and adverse reactions 
• Conduct laboratory studies on chemical safety 
• Analyze poisoning cases and toxic exposures 
• Develop safety guidelines for drug usage 
• Support regulatory and forensic investigations 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in toxicology or pharmacology 
• Strong laboratory research skills 
Work Settings 
• Toxicology laboratories 
• Pharmaceutical safety departments 
• Government regulatory agencies 
• Forensic laboratories
