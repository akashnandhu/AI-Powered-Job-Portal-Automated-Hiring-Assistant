29. Formulation Development Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Formulation Development Scientist develops new drug formulations and improves existing 
pharmaceutical products. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design drug formulations (tablets, capsules, injectables, etc.) 
• Conduct formulation experiments and testing 
• Improve stability and bioavailability of drugs 
• Perform compatibility studies 
• Collaborate with manufacturing and regulatory teams 
• Document research findings 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutics or formulation science 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical R&D departments 
• Drug development laboratories
