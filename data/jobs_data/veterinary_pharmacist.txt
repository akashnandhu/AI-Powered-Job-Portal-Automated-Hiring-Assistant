78. Veterinary Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Veterinary Pharmacist specializes in medications used to treat animals and works with 
veterinarians to ensure safe drug therapy for pets and livestock. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Dispense medications prescribed by veterinarians 
• Calculate appropriate drug dosages for different animal species 
• Compound medications specifically for animals 
• Provide guidance on veterinary drug safety and interactions 
• Manage veterinary pharmaceutical inventory 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Knowledge of veterinary pharmacology 
Work Settings 
• Veterinary hospitals and clinics 
• Veterinary pharmaceutical companies 
• Animal healthcare facilities
