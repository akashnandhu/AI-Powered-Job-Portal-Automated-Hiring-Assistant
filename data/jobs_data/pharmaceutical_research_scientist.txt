30. Pharmaceutical Research Scientist 
Job Overview 
A Pharmaceutical Research Scientist conducts scientific research to discover new drugs and 
improve existing medications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct laboratory research on drug compounds 
• Study drug interactions and pharmacological effects 
• Develop innovative pharmaceutical technologies 
• Analyze experimental data and publish findings 
• Collaborate with interdisciplinary research teams 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical research institutes 
• Drug discovery laboratories 
• Academic research centers
