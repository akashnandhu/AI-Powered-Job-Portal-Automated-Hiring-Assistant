71. AI in Drug Discovery Researcher 
Job Overview 
An AI in Drug Discovery Researcher uses artificial intelligence and machine learning 
techniques to accelerate the discovery and development of new drugs. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop AI models for drug discovery and molecular prediction 
• Analyze biological and pharmaceutical datasets 
• Identify potential drug candidates using machine learning algorithms 
• Collaborate with chemists, biologists, and data scientists 
• Improve drug discovery pipelines using computational tools 
Required Qualifications 
• M.Pharm / PhD in pharmaceutical sciences or computational biology 
• Skills in machine learning, bioinformatics, and programming 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical R&D departments 
• Biotechnology companies 
• AI healthcare startups
