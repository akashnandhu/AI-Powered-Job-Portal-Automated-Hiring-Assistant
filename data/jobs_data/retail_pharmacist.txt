17. Retail Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Retail Pharmacist works in commercial pharmacies providing prescription medications, 
OTC products, and healthcare advice to customers. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Review and fill prescriptions from doctors 
• Provide medication counseling to customers 
• Recommend OTC products for minor health conditions 
• Maintain pharmacy inventory and stock levels 
• Ensure compliance with pharmacy laws and regulations 
• Supervise pharmacy technicians and staff 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Licensed pharmacist registration 
Work Settings 
• Retail pharmacy stores 
• Supermarket pharmacies 
• Pharmacy chains
