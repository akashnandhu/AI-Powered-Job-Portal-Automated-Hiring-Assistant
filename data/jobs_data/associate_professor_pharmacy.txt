44. Associate Professor (Pharmacy) 
Job Overview 
An Associate Professor is a mid-level academic faculty member responsible for teaching 
advanced pharmacy courses and conducting research. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Teach undergraduate and postgraduate pharmacy students 
• Conduct independent research projects 
• Publish scientific articles 
• Guide postgraduate research students 
• Participate in academic committees and curriculum development 
Required Qualifications 
• PhD or M.Pharm with teaching experience 
• Research publications in recognized journals 
Work Settings 
• Pharmacy colleges 
• Universities 
• Research institutions
