16. Community Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Community Pharmacist provides healthcare services directly to the public by dispensing 
medications, offering health advice, and ensuring safe medication use. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Dispense prescription medications accurately 
• Provide counseling on medication usage and side effects 
• Advise patients on over-the-counter (OTC) medications 
• Monitor drug interactions and contraindications 
• Maintain patient medication records 
• Conduct health screenings (blood pressure, glucose checks) 
• Promote public health programs such as vaccination and smoking cessation 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD 
• Registration with Pharmacy Council 
Work Settings 
• Community pharmacies 
• Local drugstores 
• Retail pharmacy chains
