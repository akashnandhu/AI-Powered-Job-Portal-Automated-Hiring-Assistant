79. Compounding Pharmacist 
Job Overview 
A Compounding Pharmacist prepares customized medications tailored to the specific needs 
of individual patients. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Prepare customized drug formulations 
• Mix and compound medications in various forms (creams, capsules, liquids) 
• Adjust drug dosages and ingredients based on patient requirements 
• Ensure sterile and non-sterile compounding standards 
• Maintain compounding equipment and laboratory safety protocols 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / PharmD / M.Pharm 
• Training in pharmaceutical compounding techniques 
Work Settings 
• Compounding pharmacies 
• Hospitals and specialty clinics 
• Pharmaceutical compounding laboratories
