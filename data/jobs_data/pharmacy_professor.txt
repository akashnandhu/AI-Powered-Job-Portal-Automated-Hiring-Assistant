43. Pharmacy Professor 
Job Overview 
A Pharmacy Professor is a senior academic responsible for teaching pharmacy students, 
conducting advanced research, and leading academic programs in universities or pharmacy 
colleges. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Deliver lectures on pharmaceutical subjects 
• Supervise postgraduate and doctoral research projects 
• Conduct independent research in pharmaceutical sciences 
• Publish research papers in scientific journals 
• Develop academic curriculum and teaching materials 
• Mentor students and junior faculty members 
Required Qualifications 
• PhD in Pharmacy or Pharmaceutical Sciences 
• Teaching and research experience 
• Academic publications 
Work Settings 
• Universities 
• Pharmacy colleges 
• Research institutes
