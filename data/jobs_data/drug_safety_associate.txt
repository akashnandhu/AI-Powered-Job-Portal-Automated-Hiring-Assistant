39. Drug Safety Associate 
Job Overview 
A Drug Safety Associate supports pharmacovigilance teams in monitoring and evaluating 
drug safety data. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Review adverse event reports 
• Maintain drug safety databases 
• Analyze patient safety data 
• Assist in preparing safety reports for regulatory agencies 
• Ensure compliance with pharmacovigilance regulations 
Required Qualifications 
• B.Pharm / M.Pharm / PharmD 
• Knowledge of drug safety monitoring systems 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Contract research organizations (CROs) 
• Drug safety departments
