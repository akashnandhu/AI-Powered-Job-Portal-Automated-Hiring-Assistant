61. Medical Science Liaison 
Job Overview 
A Medical Science Liaison (MSL) acts as a scientific expert who communicates clinical 
research and drug information to healthcare professionals and key opinion leaders. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Provide scientific support to healthcare professionals 
• Present clinical trial data and research findings 
• Build relationships with key opinion leaders (KOLs) 
• Support clinical research and medical education programs 
• Gather feedback from healthcare professionals about products 
Required Qualifications 
• PharmD / PhD / M.Pharm 
• Strong knowledge of clinical research and drug therapy 
Work Settings 
• Pharmaceutical companies 
• Biotechnology companies 
• Medical affairs departments
